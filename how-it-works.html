HOW IT WORKS

Reading stays central.
Everything else helps it move.

Open Chapter gives reading a more energetic environment without disguising what the learner is actually doing: reading, thinking, answering and discussing.

See the lesson →
01

READ

The story gets the quietest surface.

The visual energy lives around the reading area. Text remains large, high contrast and free from unnecessary motion.

02

THINK

Prompts turn attention into understanding.

Questions direct the learner toward sequence, vocabulary, inference, character and evidence in the text.

03

ANSWER

The learner makes their thinking visible.

Responses give both learner and mentor something concrete to work with rather than reducing the session to passive reading.

04

MENTOR

A person stays beside the experience.

The mentor can clarify, encourage and challenge while leaving the reading and thinking with the learner.

THE LESSON SHELL

Immersive around the edges.
Focused in the middle.

The arcade cabinet is a frame for the learning experience, not the learning itself.
