QUESTIONS

Before you book.

How much does it cost?

$20 a month for each weekly lesson. One lesson a week is $20 a month; two lessons a week is $40 a month.

Is there a minimum or a maximum?

The minimum is one lesson a week. There's no maximum.

When are the lessons?

At a time that suits your family.

What's free?

The 30-minute trial lesson, the skills assessment, and the report and learning plan that follow.

Can we pay quarterly or yearly?

Yes. Billing is monthly as standard. Pay quarterly and save 10%, or yearly and save 20%.

What's in every lesson?

Reading, vocabulary, grammar, writing and educational games made for their age, with a qualified ELA tutor.
