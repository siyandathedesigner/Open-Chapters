Questions

How long is a lesson?

45 minutes, live and 1-on-1.

Who teaches it?

A qualified, well-vetted ELA tutor.

What's included?

Reading, vocabulary, grammar, writing and educational games made for their age, with every story played as a mission, five skills tracked, and progress reports written for parents.

Does it fit what my child does at school?

Yes. Every lesson is aligned to US state standards, so it works in step with school.

What comes first?

A skills assessment, benchmarked against K–12 ELA competencies, that shows where your child already shines.

Is it for strong readers too?

Yes. It's enrichment at every level. Strong readers get harder books, sharper questions and bigger writing challenges.
