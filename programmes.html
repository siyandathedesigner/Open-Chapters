K–12 · EVERY LEVEL

From first readers to full novels.

Every lesson grows all five skills. Here is what each grade band focuses on, aligned to US state standards.

GRADESK–2★

First books, read aloud with their tutor.

  • Phonics & decoding
  • Reading fluency
  • Retelling key details
  • Sight words
  • Context clues
  • Introduction to grammar
  • Short sentences & first stories
Start with K–2 →
GRADES3–5★

Chapter books and sharper questions.

  • Main idea & summary
  • Character & theme
  • Prefixes, suffixes & roots
  • Figurative language
  • Intermediate grammar
  • Paragraph writing
  • Narrative, opinion & report writing
Start with 3–5 →
GRADES6–8★

Theories argued with evidence.

  • Citing text evidence
  • Inference
  • Author’s purpose
  • Greek & Latin roots
  • Advanced grammar
  • Argument writing
  • Research writing
Start with 6–8 →
GRADES9–12★

Full novels, deeper arguments.

  • Literary analysis
  • Rhetoric & argument
  • Academic vocabulary
  • Style & syntax
  • Analytical essays
  • Research papers
  • Creative writing
Start with 9–12 →
