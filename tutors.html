STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Questions parents ask.

How long is each lesson?

45 minutes, live and 1-on-1 with their tutor.

How do we start?

With a free 30-minute trial lesson, then a free skills assessment, a report and a learning plan for your child.

What happens in a lesson?

Reading, vocabulary, grammar, writing and educational games made for their age.

Does it follow what they learn at school?

Yes. Every lesson is aligned to US state standards, so it works in step with school.

How will I know how they're doing?

Continuous progress monitoring across all five skills, in reports written for parents with notes from their tutor.
