INSIDE THE LESSON

What their tutor does in forty-five minutes.

  1. Picks up where the last lesson lit up.

    Every lesson is tailored to your child's growth, so their tutor starts from what they did best last time.

  2. Reads beside them, and raises the bar.

    Reading together, building vocabulary, grammar and writing, and playing games made for their age. When your child is ready for a harder question, it comes.

  3. Notes what they're noticing.

    Every lesson lands in a report written for parents, with notes from their tutor.

Beside them and never above. Raising the bar the moment they're ready.

Straight answers

Is every lesson really live and 1-on-1?

Yes. Every lesson is live, 1-on-1 and 45 minutes with a qualified, well-vetted ELA tutor.

What do tutors teach?

Reading, vocabulary, grammar, writing and educational games made for their age, aligned to US state standards.

Does my child need to be a strong reader already?

No. It's enrichment at every level. Already top of the class? Wonderful, there's so much further to go. Still waiting for their first favourite book? It's about to find them.
