# Design system

## Locked colours
- Gloss White `#FFFFFF`
- Cabinet Ink `#10131A`
- Electric Cyan `#00A7C7`
- Hot Magenta `#E0006C`
- Cabinet Violet `#3B146E`
- Signal Orange `#FF8A00`
- Vermilion `#FF3D00`
- Voltage Lime `#B6FF00`

## Typography
Archivo Black is used for large display hierarchy. Source Sans 3 is used for interface and readable copy. Story text uses a conventional serif stack to create a deliberate reading surface inside the cabinet.

## Material hierarchy
1. Gloss white page canvas.
2. High-detail rendered artwork for brand/character/environment scenes.
3. Cabinet Ink substrate for the lesson product surface.
4. Cyan/magenta/violet/orange hard edge-lighting.
5. White reading/report surfaces for comprehension and evidence.
6. Minimal glow; glow supports a physical edge rather than replacing one.

## Motion hierarchy
- Constant: restrained cabinet rail breathing and reward-star pulse.
- Pointer: tiny hero parallax and slow library image pan.
- Interaction: navigation activation, button depression, progress fill and score increment.
- Entrance: viewport reveal only.
- Reduced motion: all non-essential movement is suppressed.
